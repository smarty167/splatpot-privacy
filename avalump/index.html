Avalump Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Avalump ("the game") is made by Smarty Games. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game on Android or on the web.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and we do not require an account. Your game progress (levels, coins, skins, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The Android version shows ads through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity may collect device information such as advertising ID, device model, operating system, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Web versions on game portals may show ads provided by that portal under its own privacy policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Remote configuration

The game downloads a small public settings file (for example event and ad timing settings) from GitHub Pages. No personal data is sent.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page.

Splatpot Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

Splatpot collects no personal data itself. Ads in the Android app come from Unity Ads, and web versions use their portal's ads and analytics.

Splatpot is a free 3D physics puzzle game made by Smarty Games, an independent developer in India.

2. Data we collect ourselves: none

There is no account and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos.

Game progress (levels, coins, settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

3. Advertising in the Android app

The app shows ads through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, Unity may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device model, OS version and language
  • Ad interaction data

Read the Unity privacy policy.

Opt out of personalised ads

Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings, under Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads.

4. Web versions

When you play on CrazyGames or GameDistribution, ads and analytics are provided by those platforms under their own privacy policies.

CrazyGames policy GameDistribution policy

Splatpot is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
